Equation 39: Point Source Flux with Attenuation

Equation:

\[\phi(r) = \frac{S_p}{4\pi r^2} e^{-\Sigma_t x}\]

Variables:

  • \(\phi(r)\): Flux at distance r [1/(cm2-s)]
  • \(S_p\): Source strength [particles/s]
  • \(r\): Distance [cm]
  • \(\Sigma_t\): Total macroscopic cross section [1/cm]
    -Or, if working with photons- \(\mu_t\): Linear attenuation coefficient [1/cm]
  • \(x\): Shield or medium thickness [cm]

Description:

This equation works for both neutrons and photons as geometric and exponential attenuation operate in the same way. Flux from a point source decreases with inverse square law and exponential attenuation in the medium. Applicable for photons but with μ instead of Σ
